## THE RESEARCHER

### Understanding Intelligence Beyond Algorithms.

</div>

I am **Priyanshu Kumar Verma**, a Computer Science undergraduate interested in the science and engineering behind intelligent systems.

My interests span Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, and Autonomous Systems.

I enjoy exploring how machines learn representations, understand information, recognize patterns, and make decisions.

Rather than treating AI as a collection of ready-made tools, I prefer understanding the architectures, mathematics, optimization methods, training dynamics, and limitations behind the systems I build.

> My central question: How can we build intelligent systems that don't just produce answers, but understand, adapt, and reason?

## EXPERIMENTAL PHILOSOPHY

### Research Before Implementation. Evidence Before Claims.

</div>

I believe meaningful AI development requires more than achieving a high accuracy score.

Every experiment should answer important questions:

- What problem are we actually solving?
- Why does the selected architecture work?
- What assumptions does the model make?
- Where and why does the model fail?
- Are the evaluation metrics meaningful?
- Can the experiment be reproduced?
- What can be improved?

**My experimental workflow:**

```text
          RESEARCH QUESTION
                  |
                  v
            DATA ANALYSIS
                  |
                  v
         ARCHITECTURE DESIGN
                  |
                  v
              EXPERIMENTS
                  |
                  v
         EVALUATION & TESTING
                  |
                  v
             ERROR ANALYSIS
                  |
                  v
        ITERATION & IMPROVEMENT
                  |
                  v
            DOCUMENTATION
```

## BEYOND CURRENT RESEARCH

# KALA AI

### Building AI That Understands the Developer, Not Just the Code.

</div>

Kala AI is a long-term vision focused on developing intelligent, developer-oriented AI systems.

The philosophy behind Kala AI:

- Understand the existing codebase before making changes.
- Preserve working logic and established architecture.
- Prefer precise improvements over unnecessary rewrites.
- Make AI decisions transparent and understandable.
- Keep developers in control of their systems.
- Prioritize maintainability and engineering quality.

The objective is not simply to generate more code.

It is to build an AI engineering collaborator that understands context, respects existing systems, and makes meaningful contributions.
